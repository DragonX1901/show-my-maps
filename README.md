# SHOW MY MAPS

A Fabric mod that shows a filled map's picture without holding it — as the item icon, in
tooltips, in chests, and on the ground.

<img src="docs/images/slot-icons.png" alt="Map art drawn as the item icon" width="820">

Full details, downloads and the server-side plugin: **[Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/show-my-maps)**
